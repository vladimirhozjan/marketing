On October 9 we launched WhatsNextAction with a small core: put everything on your mind into one Inbox, decide what each thing means, and always see your next step.

Then real days started testing it. Ideas that arrive away from the desk, plans that need a place in the calendar, projects too big for a list. Every week we closed one of those gaps. One month and 33 features later, these are the ones that matter most:

An assistant that does the work
• Tell it what you want in plain words, like "move everything about the trip into a new project", and it does it, with a list of every change. It asks before it deletes anything. The models run in the EU.
• Or connect your own AI app. Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client can read and update your lists, with your permission.

Capture from anywhere
• Photo, voice and file straight into the Inbox, also when you are offline.
• On Android, share anything to WhatsNextAction from any app.

Plan and stay in sync
• Google Calendar sync and storage in your own Google Drive.
• Project templates, a brainstorm canvas for every project, and one page for your goals and areas of responsibility.
• Lists that update live on every device, and Sign in with Apple.

Every Friday a short video shows one of them in a few seconds.

Next month: WhatsNextAction goes everywhere you go. More on that soon.
