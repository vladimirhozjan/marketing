QubForge has shipped its first product: WhatsNextAction, a web app for people who keep too much in their head.

You type everything in, without sorting. The app then walks you through one question at a time until each item has a next action. What you see afterwards is one short list: what to do today, what comes next, and what you are waiting on from others.

It is built around the GTD methodology, but written for people who have never heard of it. No setup, no templates, no jargon.

Facts:
• Web app, works in any browser on phone and desktop. Native apps follow this winter.
• Free plan with no time limit. Pro at 9 € and Team at 14 € per month.
• Made in Maribor, Slovenia, by a team of one and fifteen patient beta users.
