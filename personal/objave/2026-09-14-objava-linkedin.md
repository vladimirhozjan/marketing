After more than twenty-five years of building things for other companies, it's time to build something of my own: products people rely on because they solve a real problem.

In April I founded QubForge, a small technology company in Maribor, Slovenia, with one ambition: to forge a piece of the future. Focused, intelligent software products, each built to last.

Becoming an entrepreneur is something I've wanted for a long time. I've shipped products, led teams, and watched too many good ideas die in meetings. At some point you stop waiting for the right moment and make one.

What QubForge is: a home for future-facing products. Software first, each built around one clear problem, and more to come. A very small company with a modern way of working. I'll write about it here as we go, including the parts that don't work.

Our first product ships next week. I'll let @QubForge introduce it.

If you know me from NiceHash, you know what I care about: systems people can trust, built by people who sweat the details. That hasn't changed. It's now the whole job.
